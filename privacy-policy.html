English

Updated: November 4, 2026

PetPuzzles is an animal puzzle game for children and families, published by mrsandertar. This policy covers the free game and versions with optional Premium. Purchase and Google Play Billing processing described below applies to versions that include that component; features depend on the installed version.

Game data on your device

The game keeps completed puzzles, achievements, language, music, sound and display preferences, custom puzzle choices, and Challenge routes, checkpoints and personal bests on your device. These data show progress, remember settings and let you resume a Challenge. We do not operate a server that receives your saved games or puzzle progress. Installed puzzles can be played offline.

Optional Premium and Google Play

Premium is an optional one-time purchase, without a subscription, accessed through the adult area and parental gate. Purchasing and restoring access require Google Play and an Internet connection. Google handles payment, the Google account and purchase records. The app receives limited purchase information, including the product, purchase status and purchase token, to validate the purchase and acknowledge it to Google Play; it does not receive card numbers or banking credentials. Order information available to the developer in Play Console is used for purchase administration, refunds and support.

The app remembers the last verified Premium access in private device storage for offline play. This access record uses an installation-specific security key to check its integrity and contains no purchase token, receipt or Google account details. A change in access does not by itself delete your saved puzzles or progress. Google's handling of purchase and technical data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Automatic Google diagnostics

Versions with Google Play Billing include Google's billing SDK. It handles technical information about the app, library, device and network, and billing operations, such as status and error information, and sends diagnostics to Google to operate and maintain the purchase service. Diagnostics can be queued on the device before transmission; network transfers use HTTPS.

Purchase checks run at startup and when the app returns to the foreground. This technical processing can occur without starting or completing a purchase; the parental gate protects access to purchasing and does not postpone these SDK operations. The app provides no separate switch to disable this diagnostic flow. We do not use a gameplay analytics or advertising SDK.

Children and permissions

PetPuzzles contains no advertising, social features or PetPuzzles user accounts, and does not ask children to provide a name, email address or phone number to play. Purchases are managed through the adult area. The game does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts or the photo library; the creator uses illustrations supplied with the app. We do not sell personal information. The Google processing described above remains applicable in versions with Billing.

Security, retention and deletion

Local game data remain in app-private storage until you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall it. Saved custom puzzles can be deleted individually in the app. Android cloud backup is disabled; device manufacturers' transfer mechanisms may differ. Removing app data removes the local access record; Premium access must then be verified again.

Restoring an eligible purchase with the same Google Play account restores Premium access, not deleted progress or puzzle choices. Clearing the app's data does not delete Google's purchase or diagnostic records. Google's retention, deletion options and account controls are governed by its own policies.

Support and privacy requests

Contact petmatch.support@gmail.com with privacy questions or deletion requests about information you have sent to us. If you email us, the developer and the email provider (Gmail) process your email address and message to respond and provide support. We retain that correspondence as needed for the request and applicable obligations. Do not send card details, passwords or purchase tokens. We cannot access your local game storage remotely.

This website and policy changes

This policy is hosted by GitHub Pages. Opening it sends standard web request information, such as IP address and browser information, to GitHub under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page contains no analytics, advertising or forms added by us.

If data practices change, this policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated before the change is distributed. The date above identifies this version of the policy.

Automatische Diagnose durch Google

Versionen mit Google Play Billing enthalten das Kauf-SDK von Google. Es verarbeitet technische Angaben zu App, Bibliothek, Gerät und Netzwerk sowie zu Kaufvorgängen, etwa Status- und Fehlerinformationen, und sendet Diagnosedaten an Google, um den Kaufdienst zu betreiben und zu warten. Diagnosedaten können vor der Übertragung auf dem Gerät zwischengespeichert werden; Netzwerkübertragungen verwenden HTTPS.

Vorhandene Käufe werden beim Start und bei der Rückkehr der App in den Vordergrund geprüft. Diese technische Verarbeitung kann ohne Beginn oder Abschluss eines Kaufs stattfinden; die Elternabfrage schützt den Kaufzugang und verschiebt diese SDK-Vorgänge nicht. Die App bietet keinen separaten Schalter zum Abschalten dieses Diagnoseflusses. Wir verwenden weder ein SDK zur Analyse von Spielpartien noch ein Werbe-SDK.

Käufe werden im Bereich für Erwachsene verwaltet. Das Spiel fordert keinen Zugriff auf Standort, Kamera, Mikrofon, Kontakte oder Fotomediathek an; der Puzzle-Ersteller verwendet mitgelieferte Illustrationen. Wir verkaufen keine personenbezogenen Daten. Die oben beschriebene Verarbeitung durch Google gilt weiterhin für Versionen mit Billing.

Sicherheit, Aufbewahrung und Löschung

Lokale Spieldaten verbleiben im privaten App-Speicher, bis sie gelöscht werden, die App-Daten gelöscht werden oder die App deinstalliert wird. Gespeicherte eigene Puzzle können einzeln in der App gelöscht werden. Die Android-Cloud-Sicherung ist deaktiviert; Übertragungsverfahren der Gerätehersteller können abweichen. Das Löschen der App-Daten entfernt den lokalen Zugangseintrag; der Premium-Zugang muss anschließend erneut geprüft werden.

Die Wiederherstellung eines gültigen Kaufs mit demselben Google-Play-Konto stellt den Premium-Zugang wieder her, nicht jedoch gelöschte Fortschritte oder die gelöschte Puzzle-Auswahl. Das Löschen der App-Daten entfernt keine Kauf- oder Diagnoseaufzeichnungen von Google. Deren Aufbewahrung, Löschoptionen und die Google-Kontoeinstellungen richten sich nach Googles eigenen Richtlinien.
